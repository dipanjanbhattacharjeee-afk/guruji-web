About GURUJI

GURUJI is a free classroom management application designed for private tutors and coaching institutes. Manage batches, students, fees, timetables, online classes, and study materials — all in one place.

Key Features

  • Batch & Class Management
  • Student Records & Enrollment
  • Fee Tracking & Payment History
  • Timetable & Schedule Management
  • Online Class Links with Google Meet
  • Study Materials & File Sharing
  • Announcements & Notices

Why GURUJI Requests Google Drive Access

GURUJI uses your Google Drive to store and sync your classroom data. This serverless approach means all your data stays in your own Google Drive account — GURUJI does not store any user data on external servers.

Data Privacy

Your Data, Your Control: All data is stored exclusively in your own Google Drive account. GURUJI does not collect, store, or have access to any user data on external servers.
