About GURUJI

GURUJI is a classroom management platform built to help coaching institutes and tutors manage batches, timetables, student fees, online classes, and study materials — all in one place.

Why GURUJI Uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive

Our application uses your Google account to securely authenticate users. GURUJI requests access to your Google Drive space strictly to store and manage your classroom data — batches, student lists, fees, timetables, and study materials — safely within your own Google Drive storage.

We do not use any external databases. Your data remains entirely under your control in your own Google Drive account. GURUJI cannot access your data without your explicit permission.

Key Features

  • Batch & Class Management
  • Student Records & Enrollment
  • Fee Tracking & Payment History
  • Timetable & Schedule Management
  • Online Class Links with Google Meet
  • Study Materials & File Sharing via Google Drive
  • Announcements & Notices

Data Privacy

All data is stored exclusively in your own Google Drive account. GURUJI does not collect, store, or transmit any user data to external servers. Your data, your control.
