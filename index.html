GURUJI

Private Tutor Management Platform

GURUJI is a free classroom management application designed for private tutors and coaching institutes. Manage batches, students, fees, timetables, online classes, and study materials — all in one place.

Key Features

  • ✓ Batch & Class Management
  • ✓ Student Records & Enrollment
  • ✓ Fee Tracking & Payment History
  • ✓ Timetable & Schedule Management
  • ✓ Online Class Links
  • ✓ Study Materials & File Sharing
  • ✓ Announcements & Notices

🔒 Your Data, Your Control: All data is stored exclusively in your own Google Drive account. GURUJI does not store any user data on external servers.
