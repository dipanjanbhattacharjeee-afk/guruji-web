Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Guruji ("the App") is a private tutoring management tool operated by Guruji Education ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is stored, and who can see it.

1. How Guruji stores data

Guruji has no external server or third-party database. All application data — student records, batches, fee history, announcements, and class schedules — is stored as a single JSON file inside the Tutor's own Google Drive account, in a folder named Guruji_App_Data. Google is the only infrastructure provider involved.

2. What data is collected

  • From Google Sign-In: your name, email address, and profile picture, used only to identify you as a Tutor or Student inside the App.
  • Entered by the Tutor: student name, email address, and phone number, batch assignment, and fee payment records.
  • Files you upload: question papers, study material, and student submissions, stored directly in the Tutor's Google Drive.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or payment card information.

3. Google Drive access and permissions

When a Tutor signs in, the App requests permission to create and manage files it creates on the Tutor's Drive. When a Student signs in, the App requests read-only permission so it can display schedules, files, and fee status shared by the Tutor. Neither role grants the App access to any other files in your Google account.

4. Who can see your data

Only the Tutor and the specific students the Tutor has enrolled can access data, and only the data relevant to their own batch. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Data is never used for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives in the Tutor's own Google Drive, the Tutor controls retention and deletion directly. Removing a student in the App revokes their Drive access; deleting the Guruji_App_Data folder from Drive permanently deletes all associated data.

6. Children's data

Guruji may be used to manage tutoring for minors. Student accounts are created and controlled entirely by the Tutor (e.g., a parent/guardian or the minor's own supervised Google account), and the Tutor is responsible for obtaining any necessary parental consent before enrolling a minor.

7. Your choices

You may revoke Guruji's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@guruji-edu.in.
